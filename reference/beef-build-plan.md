## Decisions already made (approved 2026-11-10)

- **Economics PDF** is not in `/reference`. Use the numbers SPEC-BEEF lists (700 lb feeder at $350/cwt, 600 lb gain at $1.02/lb, fed $2.16/lb at 1,300 lb, 62% dressing, 40% take-home, ~$825 processing, $7/$9/$11 per take-home lb). $7.10/hanging lb × 0.62 ÷ 0.40 ≈ $11/take-home lb, which matches the $11 scenario.
- **Header split:** "Buy Beef | Buy Cattle" switch. Buy Beef opens `/beef` until the beef home page lands in B7 (then `/`). Today's cattle home is also served at `/livestock`, and Buy Cattle links there. Cattle page addresses stay the same.
- **Beef config** lives in `src/config/beef.ts` (business rules); `src/config/ranch.ts` stays branding only.
- **yield.ts** functions are pure and take the config as an argument, so dashboard overrides (B6) can pass their own values. Tested with Vitest (`npm test`).
- **Take-home** is a share of LIVE weight (spec), not of hanging weight.
- **Pool dates** (deadlines, claim times, harvest and aging dates) are seeded relative to when demo data is first created, so countdowns stay meaningful after deploy. Herd ages still use `DEMO_TODAY`.
- **Mock activity** never takes the last open segment of a pool, so there is always something left to claim in a long demo.
- **Cart:** one cart with typed lines (`kind: 'lot' | 'share' | …`). B2 adds share lines with deposits through the existing cart and checkout; B5 does the full mixed-cart checkout (delivery per item type, promo codes, gift messages).
- **Herd:** animals raised for beef (`feedProgram` set) show as "Beef program" and harvested animals as "Harvested" in the herd table, and can't be put in a cattle lot.
